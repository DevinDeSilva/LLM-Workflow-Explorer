The batch SPARQL‑query extractor runs a series of query functions (e.g., `sparql_query_1_1_1`, `sparql_query_1_1_2`, …, `sparql_query_1_1_11`) that each consume an *extracts* entity and produce a corresponding **kg_info** result. The outputs include the extracted ingredient values—Chicken, Cheese, Fruit, Tuna, Olive Oil, Avocado, Sweet Potato, Yogurt, Nut, Egg, Turkey—and the structured rows returned by the queries (e.g., row 1 of `sparql_query_1_1_5` lists “Desserts”‑Yogurt with 4 g sugar; row 1 of `sparql_query_1_1_7` shows “Commercial fish”‑Tuna with NA sugar; row 1 of `sparql_query_1_1_8` contains NA values for ingredient category, name and sugar). Each execution’s *kg_info* output is then routed through the post‑processing pipeline to the program’s output port (`batch_sparql_query_extractor-kg_knowledge`).
