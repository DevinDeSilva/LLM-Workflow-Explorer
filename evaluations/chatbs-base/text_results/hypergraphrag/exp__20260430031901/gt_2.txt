The batch SPARQL Query Extractor (ID 3) runs a series of individual SPARQL queries that each produce a *kg_info* result set; the output ports of these executions are captured in the `batch_sparql_query_extractor-kg_knowledge` port and subsequently fed into the query‑result post‑processor.
