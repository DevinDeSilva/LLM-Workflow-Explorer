Artificial intelligence is used in the workflow at four program stages:
1. Generating the system‑prompt template (system_prompt_generator) – an LLM produces the prompt text.
2. Producing the user’s answer (llm_chat) – an LLM generates a response to the user prompt.
3. Extracting key entities from that answer (information_extractor) – an LLM extracts ingredient names.
